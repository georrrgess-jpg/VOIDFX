# v18 — CREEP (The Flash × Radiohead "Creep"), 9:16

**The brief (the owner):** "create a brilliant Flash edit … well planned with good transitions, custom graphics, and
custom animations … best class … a cohesive flow … smooth with excellent video quality … plenty of custom animations
and transitions … a cool hook … the loop is seamless, creative and visually pleasing and leaves the viewer asking 'how
did he do that?' … efficiency and quality."

## Concept: I WISH I WAS SPECIAL
Before the lightning, Barry Allen is an ordinary man in a cold, colourless world. The only colour in it belongs to the
speedster: a gold streak, a red suit.

- **THE HOOK (frame 0):** Barry on a night street. A gold streak tears past behind him at head height (behind his matte,
  the street smearing in its wake, a rim of its light on his edge), turns off frame and comes back across in front of him,
  low, then curves up the open side and out. It is the future Flash running back in time, passing his younger self. It
  is already moving on frame 0, so the loop lands in the middle of it.
- **"I wish I was special":** the dusk, the CCPD lab, Barry by the lamp looking at Iris. The cold look: slate blue, the
  colour drained.
- **"You're so f\*\*\*ing special":** Iris, then her hair blowing (the Flash has just passed her), then what she sees: the
  Flash, masked, his red the only colour in the frame. The red drains down out of the picture (**COLOUR DRAIN**) and he
  is Barry again, alone in the dark, lightning flickering through the window.
- **THE STRIKE (the guitar's first crunch, e28):** leaders creep down beside him and the bolt strikes on the crunch
  (**SKY CRACK**). Then **COLOUR STRIKE**: the colour floods out of the lightning into the world, outward from his chest
  along glowing veins of current, reaching his face as one soft wash.
- **The wake (second crunch, e33):** he gasps awake on a heartbeat ring from his chest (**HEARTSTART**).
- **THE DROP ("creep", e37):** a front of current climbs the frame from his feet. Under it he is the Flash; it reaches
  his chest on the drop and races up and out (**CURRENT CLIMB**). The chorus is in full colour, a new scene on every
  beat. "I'm a weirdo": Barry lowers his sunglasses.
- **EMBLEM BOLT ("what the hell"):** the real chest emblem charges. Its bolt (traced by hand on the source, placed on the
  real disc found in every frame) lifts off the disc, leaving its socket, spins once on the open side, turns face-on and
  flies at the lens as a lit gold bolt. Through it is the Speed Force, and it opens until there is nothing else.
- **"…am I doing here":** Barry in the Speed Force, its mist and lightning behind him. The mist rolls across the lens to
  Iris in tears.
- **THE TIME TEAR (the loop):** he runs back in time (the S1 time-travel run). His lightning builds and runs back across
  the frame, then tears the picture open along a bolt. The halves are pushed apart, burning at the edges. Inside the tear
  is frame 0's street, running live, where the streak behind Barry is him. It opens until it is frame 0. The end causes
  the start.

The hero is **THE TIME STREAK**: the hook, the strike's front and the loop.

## Looks
- **ORDINARY (the verse):** cold slate blue with flatter, lifted blacks; the colour is gone (16 % left). Only the
  speedster's red (the masked shot) and gold (the streak, the lightning) keep theirs.
- **SPECIAL (from the strike):** full colour, warm gold highlights, a deep red suit, teal shadows, a warm bloom.
- **The Speed Force:** the colour look with its blue kept.

## The song
- **Window:** song half-beats 148 → 228 (48.59 → 74.71 s), 80 half-beats, 26.12 s. It runs from "I wish I was special"
  to the bar before "I don't belong here".
- **Grid:** P = 0.326460 s (91.9 BPM), offset 0.2741 s, fitted on the Demucs drum stem. Even e are the beats; a bar is 8 e.
- **Lyrics** (Whisper large-v3 on the vocal stem, edit half-beat e):
  - "I wish I was special" e0.8–e8
  - "You're so f\*\*\*ing special" e14.8–e24
  - the guitar's crunches at e28 and e33
  - "But I'm a creep" e31.5–e44 (the distorted chorus from e37)
  - "I'm a weirdo" e48.4–e56
  - "What the hell am I doing here" e56–e72
- **The end:** the vocal is silent from e74. Any breath of the next line is faded off the vocal stem from e79, so the
  loop's last beat is the band alone.
