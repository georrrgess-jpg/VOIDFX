# v17 — MR. BLUE SKY (The Flash × Electric Light Orchestra "Mr. Blue Sky"), 9:16

**The brief (the owner):** "create a brand new flash edit using the attached song … try and follow the beat throughout
the song and have a new scene for each. … not include the scene with barrys eyes and electricity … many cool animations,
graphics and transitions … the best flash edit that you've created … completed in an efficient manner."

## Concept: MR. BLUE SKY (after the storm)
The Flash was made in a storm; this is the day after it.
- **SUN BREAK (frame 0):** storm clouds lit by lightning. On "Hey!" the sun burns through them and blazes out into the
  run.
- **"Running down the avenue":** a new scene on every beat, through a sunny city.
- **THE SUN (the focal piece), "see how the sun shines brightly":** the sun rises behind him, from his shoulder to behind
  his head, through his matte. The camera exposes for it, so the sky deepens. It becomes a halo: its spikes turn a step
  on every beat and its lens ghosts line up. One ghost, on his chest, swells into an iris and the next shot is inside it
  (**GHOST IRIS**).
- **"Where once was pity":** the hurt days. The last raindrop lands (**RAIN RIPPLE**), a cloud's shadow passes
  (**CLOUD SHADOW**), and the opening's storm rolls back in. It parts from the sun outward on "Mr. Blue Sky" (**CLOUD
  BREAK**). Barry looks up, and the sun opens onto him in the sunshine (**SUN OPEN**).
- **The happy days:** Flash Day with its balloons, the ball game, Barry laughing, then a Flash bolt flies at the lens
  with the Flash inside it, charged with lightning (**BOLT WINDOW**), the suit charging in the sunlit trees, the Speed
  Force.
- **EMBLEM IGNITE (the chorus; round 2):** the real chest emblem (tracked on the source) charges, lightning crawling
  round its rim. It tears free of his chest on a tether of current and coin-flips as it rises up the open side of the
  frame, clear of his face. Face-on again, it ignites: the emblem is the sun, throwing lightning down the side. The
  camera rises after it into the sky of the next shot, where it hangs.
- **LIGHTNING STRIKE (round 2), "so long":** the light dims, and a real bolt strikes beside him on the cut. The last
  shot burns in outward from the bolt's channel: the storm is coming back.
- **CLOUD OVER (the loop), "you had to hide away for so long":** the clouds roll back over the sun. They are frame 0's
  real storm: it comes over him through the gaps and closes into frame 0.

The hero is **THE SUN**: the sun break, the focal piece, the emblem, the loop (4 transitions).

**Caught by the fresh-eyes critique of the first round-2 render, fixed before delivery:**
- **Two reused clips:** packs share clips, so the same shot came from two files. The S5 forest shot was also used as
  smilelit (21.9 s), and the masked S4 close-up was both lookdown (8.2 s) and skyward (21.3 s). The later copies were
  replaced (a run with a lightning trail; the S2 suit at the money wall). A mid-frame sheet of all 33 shots now shows no
  pair.
- **The emblem's ending:** v2 ended on a flat grey disc and a stacked slide with seams. The sun is now photographic
  (disc, bloom, short spikes), drawn beside his face and never over it. The rise into the next shot is one feathered
  tilt, and the sun holds in that shot's sky, the sky exposed down round it.
- **Slides left after the owner rejected the slice wipe:** HIDE PEEK and HORIZON SLIDE are replaced. **PEEL** peels the
  frame from the corner like a sticker, its paper back curling over. **CUBE TURN** turns the frame as one face of a cube,
  the next shot on the face beside it, its edge catching the sun. The whips' two shots now meet in a band feathered
  mid-move, not a butt joint.
- **Graphics too small for a phone:** CRACKLE arcs are longer and brighter, MOTES bigger.
- **The charged shot lost him:** its `cx` pan ran the wrong way (`cx` is a start-to-end pan, not a point). It now
  follows him.
- **The strike was thin:** the bolt is thicker and brighter, and the next shot covers the width faster.

## Look: BLUE SKY
- **The grade:** high-key and clean. Skies are pushed to cerulean, highlights are sunny gold, the suit's red is rich and
  skin stays natural. The storm is in cool grey.
- **The finish:** a soft warm bloom round the light, a faint sun streak on the brightest points, fine grain.
- **The camera:** a punch on every beat (stronger on the half bar and the bar, with hit-frame blur and a colour split on
  the bar). It rests for the loop.
- **Text:** none.

## How it was made
- **Song prep:** stems, Whisper large-v3 on the vocal stem, and the grid fitted on the drum stem. The automatic window
  pick was the instrumental outro, so the window was chosen by hand on the lyrics.
- **Shots:** every Flash pack was scored for daylight and blue sky (the packs are mostly night), and the bright runs were
  contact-sheeted. pack10, which looked like daylight Flash footage, is Dexter footage and was excluded. Barry's
  lightning-eyes shot (flash_b_hq ~116.5–119.5 s) is excluded on the brief.
- **EMBLEM SUN:** the emblem is tracked per source frame (a Hough circle, smoothed; `EMB_TRACK` in the EDL).
- **Caught before the first render:**
  - **The emblem shot:** the first one was side-on and moving; it was swapped for a steady, frontal one.
  - **The sun:** the sun break first washed the storm grey, and the focal sun hid behind his cowl. It now rises from
    behind the hills, where it reads.
  - **Clouds:** they read as stucco and were rebuilt (softer octaves, billow shading, brief full cover).
  - **Framing:** the ledge and street shots framed a bystander's face.
  - **The emblem glow:** its face guard cut a hard oval.
- **Caught by the fresh-eyes critique of rev 3, fixed before delivery:**
  - **A loop of grey cloud into a grey start:** Barry and the sun now stay on screen into the last half second; the
    clouds that roll over him are frame 0's storm itself.
  - **Look-alike shots:** two front-on masked Blitzkrieg close-ups (the same tan suit) and two S1 close-ups with the
    same cut cheek; one of each was replaced (the S5 suit against the sky, the S2 suit grinning).
  - **The Blitzkrieg pack's suit reads tan:** its red is restored on his matte (never his skin).
  - **Five dissolves with two faces overlapping:** rebuilt as light fronts.
  - **The focal sun barely showed** (behind his cowl at the frame edge, and drawn as a clip-art star): it is now a
    photographic sun (a disc, bloom, six aperture spikes) rising into a halo behind his head, the sky exposed down.
  - **The hook dragged** (1.4 s shots on "Mr. Blue Sky"): two new beat cuts.
  - **The cloud cover read as grey plaster:** the clouds are the real storm footage now.
  - **Smaller:** a straight edge on the bolt crack (now a bolt), slices left on screen by the split slide, the last
    shot's framing (centred), a source cut on the first frame of the grin shot.
