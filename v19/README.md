# v19 — MEMORY REBOOT (The Flash × VØJ & Narvent "Memory Reboot"), 9:16

**The brief (the owner):** "create a brilliant flash edit … the best flash edit you have created … many custom
transitions, custom graphics and other things of that nature … high quality clips and not many clips of other side
characters … efficiency and quality."

## Concept: MEMORY REBOOT
The Speed Force keeps every moment he has lived.
- **Before the drop: his memories (the MEMORY look).** Panes of light drift in the dark behind him, each holding a frame
  of the edit. They pass behind his matte, out of focus, with a thin neon edge (cyan above, magenta below) and a glass
  sheen.
- **THE HOOK (frame 0):** the S5 suit on a wet neon street at night. A memory pane rises out of the dark behind him, with
  the night run inside it. It swings to the lens, and on the beat we fall into it (**PANE PASS**).
- **The break (the bass drops out, e16):** we dive into a pane hanging over the city (**PANE DIVE**). In the Speed Force,
  time stops, and his memories orbit him, faster and faster (**MEMORY STORM**): behind him through his matte, in front of
  him only below his chin.
- **EMBLEM REBOOT (the drop, e24):** the memories pour into the real chest emblem, tracked on the source, trailing light.
  It charges, crackling round its rim, and its bolt flares. It fires the reboot: a shock ring runs out of the emblem,
  bending the picture as it passes. Behind it is the vivid **NEON** present.
- **The drop:** a new scene on every beat, nearly all of them Barry in the suit, at night, with lightning.
- **THE LOOP:** he runs at us down the avenue. Small at the vanishing point behind him, a pane holding frame 0 (live,
  running) races toward us past him and fills the lens: his first memory, the frame the edit started on.

The hero is **THE MEMORY PANES**: the hook, the dive, the storm and the emblem's charge, and the loop.

## Looks
- **MEMORY (before the drop):** lifted blacks, colour faded to 45 %, teal shadows, peach-magenta highlights, a red
  halation round the lights, a breath of softness, a peach bloom.
- **NEON (from the drop):** deep violet-blue shadows, rich colour (skin kept natural), cyan-white highlights, a gentle
  S-curve, a magenta-cyan bloom.
