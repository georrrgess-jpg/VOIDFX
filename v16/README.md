# v16 — GOING HOME (The Flash × Drake ft. Majid Jordan "Hold On, We're Going Home"), 9:16

**The brief (the owner):** "make a flash edit using the song above. I want you to try to follow the beat in this and make
a really amazing edit. Try to make this the best flash edit you've ever made. Use custom graphics, animations,
transitions etc."

## Concept: GOING HOME
The fastest man alive can run anywhere, but every run ends at the same place.
- **The verse (drumless):** the Flash alone on a rooftop over the night city. Somewhere in those lit windows is home. He
  thinks of Iris ("I know exactly who you could be", her face rising in the night glass).
- **HOLD ON (the set piece on the drop):** "Oh, just hold on, we're going home": the shot is drawn back like a slingshot,
  straining, lightning gathering on him. The drums slam back exactly on "home" and it releases into the run.
- **The run home:** through the night city, cut on the beat, his face or the suit in every other shot.
- **WINDOW HOP (~11 s):** the camera glides into one lit window of the falling city, and he is inside it.
- **LONG EXPOSURE (the focal piece):** the shutter stays open on two runs; his lightning paints light trails that carry
  across the cut and settle into the city's own lights.
- **"It's hard to do these things alone":** the world streams past him while he stands still; lightning answers.
- **THE SIGNAL (the emblem transition):** the real Flash signal lamp (S2). We push into it; through the bolt in its
  lens is home, his face inside the bolt's blade; the bolt opens past the frame and we are in the loft.
- **Home:** the loft, Iris, Barry in warm lamplight.
- **The loop (HOME LIGHT):** the reverse of WINDOW HOP. From e74 the camera pulls back out of the loft through its
  window: the loft becomes one lit window of the night city, he comes in solid in front of it, and its light is all
  that is left: frame 0.

The hero is the HOME LIGHT (a lit window, a lamp): frame 0's windows, WINDOW HOP, the signal's lamp into the loft's lamp,
and the loop.

## How it was made
- **Song prep:** the whole song prepped (stems, Whisper large-v3 on the vocal stem, the grid fitted on the drum stem).
  The window is the last verse into the final chorus: the song's one drumless section and the drums' return on "home".
- **THE SIGNAL:** the lamp's lens is fitted per source frame (a bright-disc ellipse fit; the camera drifts about 50 px)
  and the bolt stencil in it is traced as a polygon on the source (`SIG_TRACK`, `SIG_BOLT` in the EDL).
- **Caught before delivery:** the fresh-eyes critique and the sign-off review, fixed before delivery:
  - **WINDOW HOP:** it went into the same city shot, and the window looked pasted on (a white frame, edge bands,
    mirrored texture). A different shot is now inside a warm, soft-edged window.
  - **The 9 s whip:** the library whip ran a static line through his nose and mirrored his face. It is now a custom
    DROP WHIP, and WHIP UP no longer mirrors either.
  - **HOLD ON:** its source is strobe-cut, and slow flow ghosted it. It now plays its own cadence, with a stronger
    draw-back.
  - **THE SIGNAL:** the bolt's edges cut across his face, and the glare was flat grey. His face now sits inside the
    bolt's blade, the bolt's edge glows, and the light is warm.
  - **The beam:** at first it was hidden (the face guard covered the whole close-up). Then it crossed his jaw on the cut.
    It is now kept behind his matte.
  - **The loop:**
    - the Flash faded in as a ghost, so he now appears solid on the beat;
    - two seconds were on one back shot, so the loop starts at e74 and the camera keeps moving across the wrap;
    - a partial re-render missed the loop frames (they draw frame 0's shot), so the seam failed once. They were forced
      and re-measured.
  - **Soft dissolves:** they now switch under light at its peak (headlights, lamplight, a new flare sweep). LONG
    EXPOSURE retracts into points of light. One more verse cut was added (a shot and its reverse).
